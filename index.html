EMPLOYEE WORKPLACE ENVIRONMENT QUESTIONNAIRE
Uvavanyo Lwendawo Yokusebenza
English:
The purpose of this questionnaire is to create a healthy and positive working environment for all employees of Issaba Group. We believe that a significant amount of our time is spent in the workplace, and it is therefore important that every employee feels motivated, valued, and supported when coming to work.

Please note that this questionnaire is anonymous. We encourage you to answer honestly, as we rely on your feedback to identify challenges and implement meaningful improvements within the organisation.

isiXhosa:
Injongo yolu vavanyo kukuvelisa indawo yokusebenza enempilo nenentlonipho kubo bonke abasebenzi be-Issaba Group. Sikholelwa ukuba ixesha elininzi silichitha emsebenzini, ngoko kubalulekile ukuba wonke umsebenzi azive ekhuthazekile, exatyisiwe, kwaye exhaswa xa esiza emsebenzini.

Nceda uqaphele ukuba olu vavanyo alunamagama (anonymous). Siyakukhuthaza ukuba uphendule ngokunyaniseka, kuba sixhomekeke kwiimpendulo zenu ukuze sikwazi ukuchonga iingxaki nokwenza utshintsho oluyimfuneko emsebenzini.

Section 1: General Work Environment / Icandelo 1

1. How would you describe the overall work environment? / Ungayichaza njani indawo yokusebenza?
2. Do you feel respected and valued at work? / Uziva uhlonitshwa kwaye uxatyisiwe emsebenzini?
3. Do you feel comfortable expressing your concerns? / Uziva ukhululekile ukuveza izikhalazo zakho?
4. Have you experienced bullying, harassment, favoritism or discrimination? / Wakhe wajamelana nokuxhatshazwa okanye ukungalingani?

Section 2: Management & Leadership / Icandelo 2

5. How would you rate management’s leadership? / Ululinganisa njani ubunkokheli bolawulo?
6. Do managers treat employees fairly? / Abaphathi baphatha abasebenzi ngokulinganayo?
7. Does management listen? / Ulawulo luyaphulaphula?
8. Have you felt intimidated by management? / Wakhe waziva usoyikiswa lulawulo?

Section 3: Communication / Icandelo 3

9. How effective is communication? / Unxibelelwano lusebenza kakuhle kangakanani?
10. Do you receive clear instructions? / Uyafumana imiyalelo ecacileyo?

Section 4: Policies & Fairness / Icandelo 4

11. Are policies applied fairly? / Imigaqo isetyenziswa ngokulinganayo?
12. Do you feel safe reporting issues? / Uziva ukhuselekile ukuxela iingxaki?
13. Are disciplinary actions fair? / Izohlwayo zilungile?
This questionnaire is anonymous and confidential. Responses will be used only for workplace improvement purposes.
